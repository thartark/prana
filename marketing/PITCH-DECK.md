# Prana — pitch deck outline

A slide-by-slide script for a ~10-minute investor/partner or studio-owner pitch.
Each slide: **one idea, one visual, a few words.** Speaker notes in italics. Build
it in Slides/Keynote/Pitch; pull visuals from [`./assets`](./assets) and live
screens from prana-nu.vercel.app.

> Two audiences, one deck: for **studio owners** lean on slides 2–6 and 10; for
> **investors/partners** keep 7–9 (market, model, why-now). Cut to ~8 slides for
> owners.

---

**1 · Title**
> Prana — the whole studio, in one calm app.
> *by Hayarta · prana-nu.vercel.app*
> Visual: `social-card.svg`.

**2 · The problem**
> Studio software is powerful and miserable (Mindbody), or pretty and thin.
> Owners overpay, members hate the app, and no one can see what's profitable.
> *Open with a real owner quote/vent if you have one.*

**3 · Who feels it**
> 90,000+ boutique studios in the US — yoga, Pilates, barre, cycle — mostly
> owner-operated, mostly on legacy software they resent.
> *Independent owners decide fast and feel the pain daily.*

**4 · The solution**
> One system that runs the whole studio — and that all three people actually
> like using. Visual: `how-it-works.svg` (book → run → see the numbers).

**5 · Demo (the heart of the pitch)**
> Live, not screenshots. Book a class in two taps → flip to Owner → show the
> dashboard and the profitability report → flip to Instructor.
> *Drive prana-nu.vercel.app. This slide is just "DEMO" — let the product talk.*

**6 · Why it's different**
> Calm UX members use · flat pricing, no per-transaction skim · owner reports
> (revenue/class hour, utilization, LTV) · free migration. Visual: `stats.svg`.

**7 · Market & who we sell to**
> Independent boutique studios, 1–3 locations; warmest = studios already paying
> for Mindbody. Beachhead metros: [your first 2–3 markets].

**8 · Business model**
> Flat SaaS: $129 / $199 / $349 per month, per location. No transaction skim.
> Founding rate locks early studios. *(See PRICING.md.)*
> *LTV story: studios rarely switch software — low churn once you're in.*

**9 · Why now**
> Studios are done with legacy tools post-2020; hybrid/booking expectations rose;
> members expect a two-tap, app-quality experience the incumbents can't ship.

**10 · The ask / next step**
> **Owners:** "Let me migrate your studio — free — and you go live in [X] days."
> **Investors:** the specific raise/partnership and what it funds.
> Close on the demo link: prana-nu.vercel.app.

---

**Delivery notes**
- Spend half your time on slide 5. The demo is the pitch; everything else frames it.
- One message per slide. If a slide needs a paragraph, it's two slides.
- Keep the honesty note: early, pre-logos — sell the product and the model, not
  invented traction.
- Numbers on 3, 7, 8 are illustrative — replace with your real figures before you
  present.
