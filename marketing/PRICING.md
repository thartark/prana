## Levers you can pull on a call

- **Founding rate (urgency, honest):** the first 10 studios **lock 25% off for
  life**. After that it's list price. ($129→$97, $199→$149, $349→$262.)
- **Annual (save two months):** pay for 10 months, get 12. Offer it to anyone
  who's decided.
- **Free migration** is your strongest close — it removes the #1 objection
  ("switching is a nightmare"). Lead with it when someone's on Mindbody.
- **Nonprofit / new-studio grace:** a free or half-price first 60 days for a
  brand-new studio launching on Prana. They become your case study.

## Add-ons (optional, later)

- **SMS credits** — pass-through cost for text reminders/waitlist notices.
- **Extra locations** — $99/mo each beyond the 3 in Studio+.
- **Branded member app** *(roadmap)* — your studio's name on the app store.

> Card processing runs through a standard processor (e.g. Stripe) at its normal
> rate — Prana doesn't add a markup on top. That's the "no skim" promise; say it
> plainly, because fee-heavy competitors (Momence) don't.

## How to talk about price

1. **Anchor on value first:** "What are you paying now — software *plus* the hours
   your front desk loses to it?" Then a flat monthly is easy.
2. **Never lead with the number — lead with the demo.** Let the product justify it.
3. **Frame per outcome:** "If Prana helps you save one churning membership a month,
   it's paid for itself." (Membership ~$100–$150/mo × retained months.)
4. **Use the founding rate as a real deadline**, not fake scarcity — "first 10
   studios, and I set each one up myself."
