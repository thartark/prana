# Prospecting prompts — paste-ready, one per studio type

Each prompt below is **ready to paste** into an AI **with live web access** (see the
note at the very bottom — this matters a lot). Pick the studio type you want to
call this week, set your target area, copy the whole grey block, and paste.

**Your prospects are the studios themselves** — you're selling Prana (studio
management software) to the owner. So these prompts build lists of *studios to
sell to*, not their customers.

**Setting the area (two ways):**
- **By metro/county:** change the `TARGET AREA` line to e.g. `Austin, TX` or
  `San Diego County, California`.
- **By ZIP codes:** paste a list of ZIPs into that line instead (see the dedicated
  **ZIP-code batch** prompt in §8, and the **big-markets ZIP list** at the bottom).

**Run one type + one area at a time.** It keeps the AI focused and the contact
data accurate.

## 1. Yoga studios

```
You are a B2B sales-research assistant WITH LIVE WEB ACCESS. Build me a verified
prospect list. Accuracy matters far more than length.

CONTEXT — what I sell:
Prana (prana-nu.vercel.app) is all-in-one studio-management software for boutique
studios — scheduling, memberships, waitlists, retail, staff, and finances in one
modern app. It's the calm, owner-friendly alternative to Mindbody. I sell it to
the studio OWNER.

TASK:
Build a list of 25 REAL, currently-operating businesses of this type:
  independent boutique YOGA studios (not national chains, not gyms with a yoga
  room — dedicated yoga studios)
TARGET AREA (edit this line — a metro/county, or paste a list of ZIP codes):
  Los Angeles County, California

Favor independent, owner-operated studios with 1–3 locations — they decide fast
and feel the software pain most. Skip franchises like CorePower unless it's an
independent franchisee.

OUTPUT — a markdown table with these columns:
| # | Studio | Owner / decision-maker (if findable) | Role | Phone | Email | Website | City/Area | # Locations | Current booking software (if detectable) | Why they fit (1 line) | Best opening angle for them (1 line) | Source URL |

HARD RULES:
- Only include a studio if you find real evidence it exists (its website, Google
  Business listing, or Instagram/LinkedIn). Put that link in Source URL.
- NEVER invent phone numbers, emails, or names. If you can't verify a field, write
  "unverified" — do not guess. A blank beats a fake.
- For "current booking software," check their website's "Book"/"Schedule" link —
  Mindbody links go to clients.mindbodyonline.com; also look for Momence, Walla,
  Punchpass, Glofox, Wellness Living, Acuity. If unclear, write "unknown."
- Make the "opening angle" specific to THAT studio — reference their niche, their
  neighborhood, their class styles, or their current software, not a generic line.

AFTER the table, add:
1. The 5 best directories or search queries I can use to find more of these myself.
2. Any adjacent studio types nearby that would also be strong buyers.
3. Flag any rows where you're least confident about the contact data.
```

## 5. New / opening-soon studios

The best time to win a studio is before they've trained their team on someone else.

```
You are a B2B sales-research assistant WITH LIVE WEB ACCESS. Build me a verified
prospect list. Accuracy matters far more than length.

CONTEXT — what I sell:
Prana (prana-nu.vercel.app) is all-in-one studio-management software for boutique
studios. I want to reach studios that are OPENING SOON, before they lock in
software, so I can be the system they launch on.

TASK:
Find 20 REAL boutique studios (yoga, Pilates, barre, or indoor cycling) that are
OPENING SOON or very recently opened:
TARGET AREA (edit this line — a metro/county, or paste a list of ZIP codes):
  Los Angeles County, California

Look for signals of a pre-launch or brand-new studio: "opening soon" / "now
enrolling founding members" on their site or Instagram, a recent business-license
or fictitious-business-name filing, a new Google Business listing, presale/founding
membership pages, or "coming soon" storefront coverage in local press.

OUTPUT — a markdown table with these columns:
| # | Studio | Type | Owner / decision-maker (if findable) | Phone | Email | Instagram/Website | City/Area | Opening signal (what shows they're new) | Est. opening timeframe | Best opening angle (1 line) | Source URL |

HARD RULES:
- Only include a studio with real evidence (website, Instagram, Google Business,
  a permit/license filing, or press). Put that link in Source URL.
- NEVER invent contact details. Unverifiable = "unverified." A blank beats a fake.
- Prioritize ones with a clear pre-open or <6-months-open signal.

AFTER the table, add the 5 best ways to find opening studios early (local business
journals, "coming soon" IG hashtags, chamber-of-commerce new-member lists, permit
portals), and flag your least-confident rows.
```

## Helper A — personalize outreach for one studio

Once you have a row you like, paste it into this for tailored outreach.

```
Here is a studio I'm about to contact:
  [PASTE ONE ROW: studio name, type, city, website, current software if known, any notes]

I sell Prana (prana-nu.vercel.app) — all-in-one studio-management software, the
calm alternative to Mindbody. Members book in two taps; owners get real reports
(revenue per class hour, room utilization, who's about to churn). I offer free
migration and a founding rate.

Write me, in a warm peer-to-peer voice and about their SPECIFIC studio:
1. A one-line cold-call opener that name-drops something real about them.
2. A 90-word cold email (short lowercase subject, specific, no hype).
3. A LinkedIn/Instagram DM connection note under 300 characters.
If I noted their current software, reference the switch honestly. The goal is a
free look at the demo, not a hard sell. No "revolutionary," no exclamation spam.
```

## The one thing that makes these work

**Use an AI with live web access**, or every contact will be plausible fiction.
Good choices:

- **Perplexity** — built for sourced research; best for the list prompts here
- **ChatGPT** with web search / "Search" turned on
- **Google Gemini** with search
- **Claude** with web search enabled

A plain, offline model will happily generate 25 real-looking studios with **fake
phone numbers**. Always **spot-check a few** before you reach out: click the Source
URL, confirm the studio exists, confirm the number matches.

**Also honest:** even good AIs get contact details wrong sometimes. Treat every
list as a strong starting point, not gospel — verify the phone/email on the
studio's own site, identify yourself, and honor opt-outs. When you email at scale,
follow CAN-SPAM basics (real identity, a working unsubscribe, no deceptive
subject lines).
