# START HERE

You have a full marketing folder. **Don't read all of it.** Do these four things,
in order. That's it.

---

## Step 1 — Get a list of studios to call (10 min)

Open **ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Claude with web search turned ON**. Paste the block
below. Change `[YOUR CITY]` to your city (or a nearby big one), then send.

```
You are a B2B sales-research assistant WITH LIVE WEB ACCESS. Build me a verified
prospect list. Accuracy matters more than length.

I sell Prana (prana-nu.vercel.app) — all-in-one studio-management software, the
calm alternative to Mindbody. I sell to the studio OWNER.

Find 20 REAL, independent boutique studios (yoga, Pilates, or barre) that appear
to use MINDBODY, in or near:
  [YOUR CITY]

They're on Mindbody if their website's "Book"/"Schedule" link goes to
clients.mindbodyonline.com or shows a Mindbody widget. Confirm the signal per row.

Output a markdown table: Studio | Type | Owner (if findable) | Phone | Email |
Website | Mindbody signal | Best opening angle (1 line) | Source URL

RULES: only real studios with a source link. Never invent phone/email/names —
write "unverified" instead. A blank beats a fake.
```

Copy the studios it gives you into **`prospect-tracker-template.csv`** (open it in
Google Sheets or Excel). Spot-check a couple of phone numbers on the studio's own
website before you trust the list.

*(Want a different studio type or a whole ZIP sweep? That's what
`PROSPECTING-PROMPTS.md` is for — later.)*

---

## Step 2 — Pick 5 and email them (15 min)

From your list, pick the 5 that look most frustrated / busiest. Email each one
using **Email 1** from `EMAIL-QUICK-REFERENCE.md`. The whole email:

> **Subject:** the studio software your front desk won't complain about
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> You run [Studio] — so you already know studio software is usually powerful and
> miserable, or pretty and thin.
>
> I built Prana to be both: members book a class in two taps, and you finally see
> which classes and rooms actually make money.
>
> Walk the live demo (no login — there's a Member/Instructor/Owner switcher):
> prana-nu.vercel.app
>
> — [You]

**Always include the demo link.** That link doing the work is the whole strategy.

---

## Step 3 — Post once in public (5 min)

Paste any post from **`SOCIAL-POSTS.md`** to LinkedIn or Instagram. Do this once or
twice a week. It builds trust so your emails don't land cold.

---

## Step 4 — Repeat, and only THEN open the other files

Next week: another 5 emails, follow up with the ones who didn't reply (the 12-day
cadence is in `EMAIL-QUICK-REFERENCE.md`), post again.

Reach for the rest only when you need it:
- Someone wants a leave-behind → send **`sell-sheet.pdf`**.
- Someone asks price → **`PRICING.md`**.
- A meeting or investor call → **`PITCH-DECK.md`**.
- Deeper scripts, objections, discovery → **`MARKETING-KIT.md`**.

---

**The one honest rule:** only promise what the demo actually shows. If you're not
sure a feature is live, call it "where it's going," not "what it does." You'll
never have to walk anything back.
