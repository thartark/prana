# Prana — Marketing & Sales Kit

Everything you need to talk about Prana and sell it: the story, who to call, what
to say, what to send, and how to handle pushback. Copy/paste freely and make it
your own.

> **Who you sell to:** owners of independent boutique studios — yoga, Pilates,
> barre, cycle, and small fitness — who are either overpaying for Mindbody and
> hate it, or duct-taping a booking app + spreadsheets + a payroll tool together.
> Prana runs the whole studio in one calm, modern app that members, instructors,
> and owners actually enjoy using.
>
> **Live demo (no login):** [prana-nu.vercel.app](https://prana-nu.vercel.app) —
> use the **Member / Instructor / Owner** switcher in the demo banner to walk all
> three views.

## 1. The one thing to remember

**Studio software is either powerful and miserable (Mindbody) or pretty and thin.
Prana is the first one that's both — the whole studio in one calm app that
members love, staff can run, and owners can finally see the numbers in.**

Everything below is just this sentence, said different ways to different people.

## 3. What the demo actually shows (proof, honestly)

Everything here is real in the live demo today — show it, don't just claim it:

- **A schedule members actually use.** `/classes` is a week grid: date scrubber,
  filter by style / level / instructor / time, spots-remaining on every row,
  pricing per row, and **two-tap booking with no page reload**. Waitlist when full.
- **The full owner back-office.** A grouped admin covering scheduling, series &
  courses, retreats, teacher training, memberships & tiers, discounts, corporate,
  retail, finances, staff, clients, and settings — every screen populated with
  realistic sample data.
- **Owner reports owners actually ask for.** Revenue per class hour, room
  utilization %, instructor fill rate, class profitability (margin + $/hr), and
  LTV by acquisition source.
- **Three roles, one click.** The demo banner's Member / Instructor / Owner
  switcher lets a prospect feel each seat.
- **A homepage that converts** like a real studio site — intro offer, this-week
  schedule, first-visit info.

> **Honesty note (read this):** Prana is early and you have **no paying studios or
> testimonials yet** — that's normal. Don't fake them, and don't demo features
> that aren't in the build. Sell the *experience in the demo* and the *owner math*
> (below). Some deeper mechanics — auto-charging no-show fees, payroll that
> computes from attendance, dunning sequences — are on the near roadmap; describe
> them as "where it's going," not "what it does today," and you'll never get
> caught overselling. Your first two or three studios become your proof.

## 4. Who to call, and the angle for each

Start with **independent owner-operators** — one to three locations. They feel the
pain daily and can say yes without a committee.

**Studios currently on Mindbody (your best target)**
> "You're already paying for studio software — the question is whether your
> members and front desk actually like using it. Prana is the same capability
> without the 2009 feel, and I can move you over without losing your history.
> Want to see the member booking flow side by side with what you have?"

**New studios opening in the next 90 days**
> "The best time to pick your software is before you open, not after you've trained
> the whole team on something clunky. Prana gets you a bookable schedule,
> memberships, and a front desk on day one — want to launch on it?"

**Studios running on spreadsheets + a cheap booking app**
> "You've outgrown the duct tape. The moment you're tracking payroll in a
> spreadsheet and chasing no-shows from memory, you're leaving money on the table.
> Prana pulls it into one place — and shows you which classes actually pay."

**Multi-location / small chains**
> "Managing two or three locations means you need one schedule, one membership
> base, and one set of numbers — not three logins. Prana is built for that. Let
> me show you the owner reports across rooms and instructors."

## 5. Cold call script

**Opener (permission-based, disarming):**
> "Hey [Name], it's [You] — we've never spoken so I'll be quick, and you can tell
> me to buzz off. Got 30 seconds?"
>
> *(almost everyone says yes to that)*

**The hook (make it about them):**
> "You run [Studio], right? Quick question — what are you using to run booking and
> memberships today, and on a scale of one to Mindbody, how much do you love it?"
>
> *(let them vent — they will)*

**The turn:**
> "That's exactly why I called. I build Prana — it's the whole studio in one app,
> but it's actually pleasant to use. Members book in two taps, and you finally get
> real numbers like revenue per class hour and who's about to churn. Same power as
> Mindbody, a fraction of the friction."

**The ask (low commitment):**
> "I'm not going to sell you on a call. Can I send you a link to walk the live demo
> yourself — there's a switcher so you can see the member, instructor, and owner
> views? If it clicks, we talk. If not, no harm."

**If yes:** get their email, send the demo link + short recap (template below)
within 5 minutes while it's warm.

## 6. Discovery questions (use on any call)

- "What are you running the studio on today — and what made you pick it?"
- "What's the thing about it that annoys you or your front desk the most?"
- "How do your members feel about the booking app? How many support texts do you
  field a week because of it?"
- "How do you handle no-shows and late cancels right now? Do you actually collect?"
- "When someone hasn't been in for three weeks, how do you know — and what happens?"
- "How do you run instructor pay today? How long does it take each pay period?"
- "If you had to name your most and least profitable class right now, could you?"
  *(almost no one can — that's the owner-reports opening)*
- "What would switching cost you in time and nerves? What would make it worth it?"

## 7. Objection handling

**"We already use Mindbody / [competitor]."**
> "Most studios I talk to do — and most have a love-hate thing with it, heavy on
> the hate. I'm not asking you to rip anything out today. Just walk the demo and
> compare the member booking flow to yours. If yours is better, keep it. If Prana
> is calmer and shows you numbers you don't have today, that's worth a look."

**"Switching is a nightmare / I don't have time."**
> "Totally fair — switching software mid-season is nobody's idea of fun. That's
> exactly why we handle the migration and stand up your schedule, members, and
> pricing for you. Your job is to look at the demo and tell me if it's better.
> Ours is to make the move painless."

**"Is it as powerful as Mindbody?"**
> "Same surface area — scheduling, memberships, waitlists, retail, staff,
> finances, reporting. The difference is what it *feels* like to use, and that
> owners get profitability and retention numbers Mindbody buries. Walk the admin
> in the demo and judge the depth yourself."

**"How do I know members will actually use it?"**
> "Open the demo on your phone and book a class — two taps, no reload, no account
> maze. That's the whole point. Members don't call you when the app just works."

**"You're early — do you have other studios on it?"**
> "I'm early and honest about it — you'd be one of the first, which is why the
> founding rate is locked for life and I'll personally handle your setup. What I
> *can* show you is the product itself: here's the live demo, every screen, real
> data. Judge it on that, not on a logo wall."

**"It's not in the budget."**
> "What are you paying now, all-in — software plus the hours your front desk loses
> to it? Prana is usually less than the tool *and* the aspirin. And there's a
> founding rate locked for life if you're one of the first in."

**"I need to think about it."**
> "Makes sense. The demo costs nothing and takes five minutes — can I send it so
> you're deciding with the actual product in front of you instead of my pitch?"

## 13. Pricing conversation

> **These are suggested/illustrative numbers — set your real prices before you
> quote anyone.** Boutique-studio tools typically run ~$130–$600/mo (Mindbody),
> and fee-heavy models (Momence) skim every transaction. Prana's angle is
> **flat, transparent pricing with no per-transaction skim.**

- **Anchor on value, not cost:** "What are you paying now, plus the hours your
  desk loses to it?" Then a flat monthly is easy.
- **Lead with the demo** to remove risk: "Walk it first, decide with the product
  in front of you."
- **Suggested ladder** (adjust to your market):
  - **Starter** — solo / one location, core booking + memberships.
  - **Growth** — one location, full admin + owner reports + retail.
  - **Studio+ / Multi** — 2+ locations, per-location pricing.
- **Founding rate as honest urgency:** "First 10 studios lock their rate for life
  and I handle setup personally."
- **Annual saves two months** — offer it to anyone who's decided.
- **Free migration** is your strongest close — it removes the #1 objection.

## 15. Selling with no customers yet (your actual situation)

1. **Sell the product, not the brand.** The live demo is the proof. Show, don't
   claim — and let them drive it themselves with the persona switcher.
2. **Give the demo every time.** Zero risk; the product sells itself the moment an
   owner books a class on their own phone.
3. **Be first-customer honest.** "You'd be one of the first — that's why the rate
   is locked and I set you up myself." Early adopters *like* being early.
4. **Make migration your moat.** Owners don't switch because switching hurts.
   Offering to do it for them, for free, beats every feature bullet.
5. **Turn studio #1 into the case study.** The day an owner says "my front desk
   stopped complaining," get one sentence in writing. That sentence sells #10.
6. **Publish in public.** Post the owner-reports screen, the two-tap booking clip.
   Being the person who *gets* studio economics builds authority before logos do.

## 17. Building your prospect list

To find studios to call, use the companion file
**[`PROSPECTING-PROMPTS.md`](./PROSPECTING-PROMPTS.md)** — paste-ready prompts you
drop into an AI *with live web access* (Perplexity, ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude with
search) to build verified lists of studios by type and area. It includes:

- One prompt per studio type — yoga, Pilates, barre, cycle/boutique fitness,
  new/opening-soon, multi-location, and **studios already on Mindbody** (your
  warmest list).
- A **ZIP-code batch** prompt with a paste block for sweeping specific ZIPs.
- A **big-markets ZIP list** (LA, SD, NYC, Austin, Denver/Boulder, etc.) to seed it.
- Helpers to verify an owner, **detect what software a studio runs today**,
  personalize outreach for one studio, and pick which market to work first.

Rule of thumb: prospects on expensive legacy software (Mindbody) are the warmest;
new/opening studios are the easiest first-time wins.

**Track your outreach** in [`prospect-tracker-template.csv`](./prospect-tracker-template.csv)
— open it in Sheets/Excel, drop the AI's results in, and use the Status /
Last Touch / Next Touch columns to run the 12-day cadence in §11.
