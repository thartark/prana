# Cold email — quick reference

The whole outreach sequence on one screen. Full versions live in
[`MARKETING-KIT.md`](./MARKETING-KIT.md) §8. Keep subjects lowercase and specific;
keep bodies under ~90 words; one clear ask (walk the demo).

## The sequence at a glance

| Day | Email | Subject line | The one job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | **1 · Opener** | `the studio software your front desk won't complain about` | Frame the pain, drop the demo link |
| 3 | **2 · Follow-up** | `re: the software your front desk won't complain about` | "Book a class on your phone — that's the pitch" |
| 5 | **3 · Value nudge** | `which of your classes actually makes money?` | Point at the owner report |
| 5* | **4 · Recap** | `the demo link I mentioned` | *Send within 5 min of a good call* |
| 12 | **5 · Breakup** | `closing the loop` | Graceful exit, door left open |

\* Email 4 is call-triggered, not day-triggered.

## Rules that keep them working

- **One ask per email:** walk the demo. Never stack CTAs.
- **No hype words** — no "revolutionary," "game-changer," "!!!". Peer-to-peer tone.
- **Make line 1 about them,** not you ("You run [Studio]…").
- **The link is the payload:** `prana-nu.vercel.app` (or `/classes`, or
  `/admin/reports` for the value nudge).
- **Personalize with the current-software angle** when you know it — Helper A in
  [`PROSPECTING-PROMPTS.md`](./PROSPECTING-PROMPTS.md) writes it for you.
- **Compliance:** real identity, real reply-to, a working unsubscribe, no
  deceptive subjects (CAN-SPAM basics). Honor opt-outs immediately.

## Subject-line swipe file (steal & test)

- `the studio software your front desk won't complain about`
- `which of your classes actually makes money?`
- `a calmer alternative to [their current software]`
- `2 taps to book — open this on your phone`
- `moving off Mindbody without the nightmare`
- `[Studio] + a schedule your members will actually use`
- `free to switch, free to look`
